Campaigns running on both channels
Pair an email campaign with the LinkedIn campaign working the same people — that is the only manual step. Every 15 minutes the dashboard reads both platforms, matches everyone, and automatically stops the email of anyone who already answered on LinkedIn. The one thing it cannot do alone is remove an email-replier from LinkedIn (that platform has no stop endpoint), so it keeps that short list ready for you instead.
What happens when someone engages
Every hour this reads the posts your sending profiles have scraped, brings in everyone who liked or commented, files each person, writes their message, looks up an email address, and — while the automatic switch is on — puts them into a LinkedIn campaign and an email campaign at the same time. Nothing is sent from here: each platform sends only while its own campaign is live.
